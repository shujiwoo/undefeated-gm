      How to Play

      Classic Draft

      Spin a random franchise and era. Pick any player who played for that franchise in that era, then place them into an eligible position.

      Positions

      Players can be placed at any position listed for them. Drag placed players to move or swap if both players are eligible.

      Respins

      You get one team respin and one era respin for the whole run. Use them carefully.

      How Team Success Is Calculated

      The game does not only look at per-game stats. PPG, RPG, APG, SPG, and BPG matter, but the simulator also checks skill ratings, era-average production, awards/accolades, role, position fit, spacing, defense, playmaking, rebounding, roster depth, and chemistry.

      • Era-average stats: decade modes use the player's average production for that team in that era, not just their single best season.
      • Skill ratings: scoring, shooting, playmaking, defense, rebounding, athleticism, clutch, and durability help decide how strong a player really is.
      • Accolades: MVPs, All-NBA, All-Defense, championships, award shares, and star-level seasons add value.
      • Fit: a balanced team with shooting, defense, passing, size, and rebounding can outperform a team of players with better box-score stats but poor chemistry.
      • Randomness: if simulation randomness is on, the final record can swing slightly so the same team does not always finish with the exact same result.

      Scores and Records

      After each run, your record, grade, roster, and mode result can be saved locally in Records. Records are stored on your device/browser.

      Other Modes

      Franchise Mode locks your team. Battle Mode plays a best-of-seven. Playoff Mode simulates four rounds. Dynasty Mode runs five years. GM Trade Mode lets you accept offers before simulating.
